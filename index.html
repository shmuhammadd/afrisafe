Making AI safe for Africa.

AfriSafe is a research group for AI safety, alignment and education in Africa. We find where AI models fail African users, study how to align them with African languages and cultures, and teach Africans to use AI safely.

  • Read our research

Our focus

AI safety

We test AI models in African languages and publish where they fail.

Alignment

We study whether models respect African cultures, and whether they behave in African languages as they do in English.

Our research areas

Red teaming in African languages

We test models in African languages. Native speakers write the prompts, so the tests use real slang, proverbs and code-switching.

Cultural alignment

We measure how well models know everyday life across African communities, and where their answers carry another culture's assumptions.

Safety benchmarks

We build open test sets for hate speech, toxicity and harmful advice in African languages, labelled by people who speak them.

Alignment of multilingual models

We study why safety training in English fails to carry over to other languages, and test methods that close the gap.

Policy and governance

We write briefs for African regulators who must decide how AI systems are tested before they reach the public.

Training new researchers

The AfriSafe Fellowship takes people who know these languages and teaches them to test AI systems.

The AfriSafe Fellowship

Three months, paid and remote. Train as an AI safety researcher, working on your own languages.

Register interest and we will email you

  1. 1. Apply

    Send a short application and a research idea. We look for people who know an African language and can code.

  2. 2. Train

    Study red teaming, evaluation and alignment through taught sessions and paper readings.

  3. 3. Research

    Run a project with a mentor. Test a model in your language and record what breaks.

  4. 4. Publish

    Release the paper, the dataset or the benchmark openly, so labs and regulators can use it.

Frequently asked questions

Ask us another question
What is AfriSafe?

AfriSafe is a research group that works on AI safety, alignment and education for Africa. We test AI systems in African languages, study how to align them with African cultures, train new researchers through a fellowship, and teach free AI courses. AfriSafe is independent. It is not part of Imperial College London, where our founder works as a researcher.

What does AfriSafe research?

Five areas: red teaming in African languages, cultural alignment, safety benchmarks, the alignment of multilingual models, and policy. See our research areas and read our papers.

Which languages do you work on?

We work on all African languages, such as Hausa, Swahili, Yoruba, Igbo, Nigerian Pidgin and Amharic, among others.

Who is the fellowship for?

Students and early-career researchers interested in AI safety.

Is the fellowship paid, and how long is it?

Yes, fellows are paid. The fellowship runs for three months and is remote, so you can take part from anywhere.

When do fellowship applications open?

We will announce the date on this page. Write to contact@afrisafe.ai and we will email you when they open.

How can my organisation work with AfriSafe?

You can collaborate on a research project, mentor a fellow, support our research, or hire us to red team your model in African languages. See how to work with us.
